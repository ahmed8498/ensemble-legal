Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 6, 2026

Ensemble ("we," "our," or "us") is a mobile application that helps you journal your personal style. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide the app's features:

  • Account information — your email address and display name, provided during registration.
  • Google profile data — if you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email, and profile picture from Google.
  • Apple ID data — if you sign in with Apple, we receive the name and email you choose to share.
  • User-created content — outings (title, date, location, description), wardrobe items (name, category, photos you upload), and people names you add to your circle.
  • Purchase information — if you subscribe, your subscription status and purchase history from the App Store or Google Play, processed through RevenueCat. Payment itself is handled by Apple or Google; we never see your card details.
  • Support messages — what you write in Contact support, with your app version and phone system version, so we can answer it.

We do not collect device identifiers, location data, usage analytics, or any data beyond what is listed above.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to:

  • Create and maintain your account.
  • Store and display your outings, wardrobe items, and people back to you.
  • Authenticate you when you sign in.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on Supabase, a cloud infrastructure provider. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest (AES-256). We use industry-standard security practices to protect your information, but no system is 100% secure.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal data, and we do not use advertising networks or analytics services. We share data only with the service providers that run Ensemble, and only what each one needs: Supabase stores your account and content; RevenueCat processes subscriptions and receives your account ID and your purchase history from the App Store or Google Play; and Resend delivers the support messages you send, by email, to our support inbox at Google (Gmail). These providers process the data only to provide their service to us.

5. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, all of your data — including outings, wardrobe items, people, and account information — is permanently removed from our servers.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data at any time through the app.
  • Edit your display name and content within the app.
  • Delete your account and all associated data via Profile > Delete Account in the app.
  • Contact us with any privacy-related questions.

7. Children's Privacy

Ensemble is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with their data, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes become effective when posted on this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Your continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
